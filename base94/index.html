The problem

Most small businesses don’t get hacked because of advanced attacks.

They get compromised through simple issues like:

  • Weak or reused passwords
  • Missing multi-factor authentication
  • Former employees still having access
  • No backup or recovery process
  • Misconfigured email accounts
