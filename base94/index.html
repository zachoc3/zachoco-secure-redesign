The problem

Most small business disruptions don’t come from advanced hackers.

They come from simple issues like:

  • Someone gaining access to an employee’s email
  • Former employees still being able to log in
  • No reliable way to recover lost data
  • Mistakes that expose customer or financial information

What this is

Business protection in plain English

Base94 helps small businesses understand how their business could be disrupted and what needs to be fixed first. We focus on:

What could stop your business from operating normally

Email takeover, locked files, or systems you can’t access when you need them.

What puts customer or financial data at risk

Situations where sensitive information could be lost, stolen, or shared by mistake.
